A PERSONAL WELCOME

A vision shaped by care, learning, and faith.

Founder & Chief Wellness Officer · Welcome Message

At The Vine Wellness Institute, we believe that true healing begins with understanding the whole person.

Our multidisciplinary team of medical professionals, wellness practitioners, and holistic health experts work collaboratively to provide personalized health assessments, evidence-based care, and comprehensive wellness programs.

Integrating advanced medical technologies with the wisdom of natural healing, we offer a thoughtful and individualized approach to health optimization, longevity, and transformative living.

Nestled in the serene natural beauty of Tabanan, Bali, The Vine Wellness Institute is a sanctuary where science, nature, and compassionate care come together.

Our carefully curated wellness programs are inspired by the six pillars of Lifestyle Medicine—whole-food nutrition, physical activity, restorative sleep, stress management, positive social connection, and avoidance of harmful substances.

Through personalized nutrition, therapeutic movement, restorative experiences, and integrative health interventions, we empower each individual to take an active role in their health journey.

More than a wellness destination, The Vine is a place to restore balance, renew vitality, and cultivate a deeper connection with oneself and nature.

Our mission is to help people live healthier, longer, and more meaningful lives—where evidence-based medicine meets the wisdom of nature, and every journey is guided by the belief that the One who created us also holds the ultimate source of healing.
