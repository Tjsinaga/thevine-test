A HEALING STORY

A Journey of Faith, Hope, and Healing

Preschelle, 37Philippines | 2023Cancer Survivor & Wellness Program Participant

In July 2023, I was diagnosed with active cancer. I was advised to undergo surgery as soon as possible because of concerns that the cancer could spread rapidly.

I was devastated. I felt that I was still too young to die. I couldn’t bear the thought of leaving my husband so soon. I had even given up the opportunity to work abroad because I had promised my parents that I would take care of them. Yet suddenly, I was facing the possibility that I might leave this world before them.

Despite everything, my family and I held on to hope and continued praying for a miracle.

My journey toward wellness began when my mother told me about Ms. Khristine Pineda, who is a Naturopathic practitioner and was practicing a holistic wellness program in Indonesia. My husband and I, together with our family, were also hoping and praying to have a baby someday.

With faith and hope in my heart, I decided to go to Indonesia and joined the wellness program and embraced a healthier lifestyle.

Today, I am grateful to share that my latest laboratory and medical test results have returned to normal, and I feel healthier and inspired each day.

This experience has deepened my faith in God and inspired me to encourage others to embrace healthy living. I now share what I have learned with other people, hoping that my journey will inspire them to take better care of their health and appreciate the healing wisdom found in nature.

I praise and thank God for His goodness, guidance, and the blessings He has given me throughout this journey.

Even my family members have now embraced a healthier lifestyle!

My testimony is a reminder that there is always hope, even in the most difficult moments of life.

The God who created us is the God who heals. 🌹

With gratitude and praise to God,

Preschelle, 37Philippines | 2023Cancer Survivor & Wellness Program Participant

This is a personal account and is not medical advice or a promise of results. Wellness programs do not replace diagnosis or treatment from a qualified healthcare professional.
